Built for how shops here actually work

The things that slow a counter down, handled.

Keeps selling with no network

When the internet drops, the till carries on. Each sale is kept on the phone and sent up by itself the moment you are back online, with nothing recorded twice.

One button to charge and print

No menus to hunt through. One tap saves the sale and sends the receipt to your Bluetooth thermal printer, 58 mm or 80 mm.

Install it like an app

Open uzaa.co.ke once and add it to the home screen. It opens full screen, starts fast, and there is nothing to download from Google Play.

Sell by litre, kilo or piece

Milk in litres, sugar in kilos, oil by the half litre. Each item carries its own unit and takes decimal quantities, so 0.5 is a normal sale.

Your own categories

Group what you sell the way you think of it, and tap a category at the till to find things fast. You add and rename them yourself in Settings.

Credit, recorded properly

Switch on credit sales and Credit becomes a payment method. Uzaa keeps the name, the number, what they took and what they owe, takes part payments, and opens WhatsApp with the reminder ready.

Receipts your way

Print every time, ask each time, or never print at all. The sale is always saved either way.

Every sale out to Excel

Pick any dates and download one file with the totals, the payment methods and every item sold. Good for your accountant and for KRA.

Barcodes only if you use them

Scan with a handheld reader, or switch barcodes off completely and work by name. The screen changes to match.

Questions

What shop owners ask first.

What happens when the internet goes?

You keep selling. Uzaa saves each sale on the phone and uploads it by itself once you are back online, and nothing is recorded twice. Printing and credit sales need a connection, so a credit sale waits until the network is back.

Do I have to download it from Google Play?

No. Open uzaa.co.ke once and install it from the browser. On Android that is the menu, then Install app. On iPhone it is Share, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens full screen with its own icon.

Can I give goods on credit?

Yes, if you switch credit sales on in Settings. Credit becomes a payment method at the till, you pick the customer, and Uzaa keeps what they owe. You can set the most one person may owe, take part payments, and send a WhatsApp reminder.

I sell by litre and by kilo. Does that work?

Yes. Each item carries its own unit and accepts half and quarter amounts, so half a litre of milk or one and a half kilos of sugar is a normal sale.

Can I use my own printer or scanner?

Most Bluetooth thermal receipt printers and handheld scanners work, on 58 mm or 80 mm paper. Bring the model name to the demo and we will confirm it before you buy anything.

Can I run more than one branch?

Yes. Each branch has its own stock, its own cashiers and its own receipts. You see every branch together in your reports.

What can a cashier see?

Only the Sell screen and their own sales, in their own branch. They cannot change prices, stock, staff or settings.

How do customers pay?

On Uzaa Standard, your cashier records Cash, M-Pesa or Card at the till. On Uzaa Pay, customers can scan a QR code to pay.

How is billing done?

Every three months, at the plan price. Hardware is billed once, separately.
